# Canonical job scope

The initial library has 50 small canonical objectives. Twenty-five related acceptance scenarios are retained as data variants rather than separate bespoke agents. A variant must be explicitly selected, its extra requirements assessed and its own assertions used. Variants are not additional canonical jobs and do not inflate coverage.

The three primary patterns are Process Automation, Worker Augmentation and Enterprise Intelligence, following the supplied Dataiku guide as taxonomy input. Domain categories describe the work; governance, recovery, parallel and qualification labels describe Agent Control-specific concerns. No proprietary agent implementation is reproduced.
