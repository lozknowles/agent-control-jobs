Pages 12–19 describe eight illustrative workloads: ticket management, invoice prioritisation, maintenance scheduling, sales assistance, trial-site intelligence, compliance monitoring, supply-chain resilience and client relationship intelligence. These are proposed architectures, not independently demonstrated deployments. Source-page references are in use-cases.yaml.

Page 15 visually confirms inconsistent invoice-processing bullets under the sales-assistant heading. Preserve this uncertainty rather than silently repairing the source.

Pages 3–4 distinguish simple fixed rules from work requiring contextual judgment. Our deterministic fixtures measure components and negative controls; they do not establish that an agent is necessary. End-to-end cases require varied inputs, multiple evidence sources, real integrations and independent judgment.

Page 12 places approval before customer responses. Pages 13, 18 and 19 describe record changes and communications: the library declares these as future integration boundaries, with no inherited authority. Clinical trial selection, retail optimisation and physical controls remain unsupported as complete workflows.
